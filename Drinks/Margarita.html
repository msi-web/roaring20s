Margarita

A Simple Classic

How to Make a Margarita

1

Gather Your Ingredients

You'll need 2 oz of tequila, 0.75 oz of triple sec, 0.75 oz of fresh lime juice, and 0.75 oz of simple syrup. Quality ingredients make all the difference!

2

Combine in Shaker

Pour 2 oz of tequila, 0.75 oz of triple sec, 0.75 oz of lime juice, and 0.75 oz of simple syrup into a cocktail shaker.

3

Add Ice & Shake

Fill the shaker with ice and shake vigorously for 10-15 seconds until the shaker is frosty and cold.

4

Strain & Serve

Strain the mixture into a salt-rimmed rocks glass filled with fresh ice. Garnish with a lime wheel and serve immediately!
