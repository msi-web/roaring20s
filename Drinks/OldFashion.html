Old Fashion

A Timeless Classic

How to Make an Old Fashion

1

Add Sugar & Bitters

Place a sugar cube (or ½ oz simple syrup) into a rocks glass. Add 2-3 dashes of Angostura bitters. Gently muddle or stir until the sugar dissolves.

2

Add Whiskey

Pour 2 oz of bourbon into the glass. Stir well to combine all ingredients.

3

Ice It Up

Add a large ice cube or several standard ice cubes to the glass if desired. Stir gently for about 30 seconds to chill.

4

Garnish & Serve

Express an orange peel over the drink and drop it in. Add a cherry if desired. Serve immediately and enjoy!
