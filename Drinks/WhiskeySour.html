Whiskey Sour

A Sour Classic

How to Make a Whiskey Sour

1

Gather Your Ingredients

You'll need 2 oz of bourbon, 0.75 oz of fresh lemon juice, and 0.75 oz of simple syrup. Quality ingredients make all the difference!

2

Combine in Shaker

Pour 2 oz of bourbon, 0.75 oz of lemon juice, and 0.75 oz of simple syrup into a cocktail shaker.

3

Add Ice & Shake

Fill the shaker with ice and shake vigorously for about 10-15 seconds until the shaker is frosty and cold.

4

Strain & Serve

Strain the mixture into a rocks glass filled with fresh ice. Garnish with a cherry or lemon wheel and serve immediately!
