Tom Collins

A Tall Classic

How to Make a Tom Collins

1

Gather Your Ingredients

You'll need 2 oz of gin, 0.75 oz of fresh lemon juice, 0.75 oz of simple syrup, and 0.5 to 1 oz of soda water. Quality ingredients make all the difference!

2

Combine Ingredients

Pour 2 oz of gin, 0.75 oz of lemon juice, and 0.75 oz of simple syrup into a tall glass. Stir well to combine.

3

Add Ice & Soda

Fill the glass with ice cubes and top with 0.5 to 1 oz of soda water. Stir gently for about 30 seconds to chill.

4

Garnish & Serve

Garnish with a cherry and lemon wheel. Serve immediately and enjoy this refreshing tall drink!
